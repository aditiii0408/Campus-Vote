# CampusVote

Based on the ui-ux-pro-max design-system search for a trustworthy, light campus voting dashboard. Use minimalist Swiss styling: spacious grid, white cards, light slate background, restrained blue primary actions, subtle borders, and no dark theme. Adapt the search typography to Manrope headings and DM Sans body for a friendly campus identity. System sans-serif fallback works offline.

Primary #315EE7; text #202B3D; secondary text #647087; background #F7F9FC; cards #FFFFFF; border #E5E9F0. Sage indicates verification, muted peach and lavender distinguish candidates with names and initials as redundant labels.

Use semantic buttons, visible focus rings, native modal focus handling, reduced-motion support, labeled voting states and meaningful empty results. Desktop sidebar becomes a compact top navigation on mobile. Never imply preview votes are blockchain transactions. Never claim one wallet verifies a student's identity or ballot secrecy.
